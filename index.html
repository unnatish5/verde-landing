01 / The problem

The problem

Sustainability information is everywhere, but understanding it is surprisingly difficult. Company disclosures, product claims, carbon estimates and everyday choices rarely connect. And when the underlying data is uncertain, that uncertainty often disappears behind a neat score.

02 / The solution

The solution

Verde brings company sustainability records, product intelligence and your everyday behavior into one place. It doesn’t tell you to stop buying things or prescribe the ‘right’ way to live. It shows you the evidence and lets you decide.

03 / A connected picture

What Verde does

Company Sustainability Passports

Standardized brand profiles across climate, materials, water, waste, supply chain and transparency, with every claim labeled by evidence quality.

AI Sustainability Diary

Write ‘Took the bus 8 km instead of an Uber’ and Verde estimates the impact, with no questionnaire.

Personal Impact Dashboard

See how you’re doing against your own baseline, spot category trends and find realistic ways to improve.
